<p>Vortex brings your Discord presence together in one Android dashboard. Share app and media activity, choose a console game preset or create your own custom presence.</p>
<p>Search the game catalog, save your last console preset, and create a manual game and platform preset when the catalog is unavailable. Choose the permissions and presence mode that work for you.</p>
<p>Vortex is an unofficial client. Discord connectivity and third-party catalog availability are not guaranteed.</p>
